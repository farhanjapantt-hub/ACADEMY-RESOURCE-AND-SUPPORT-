Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swapzy – Currency & Units ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app on your Android device. Swapzy is developed by Fahad Ali. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Overview

Swapzy is a unit and currency converter. It converts length, weight, area, volume, temperature, speed, time, data storage, pressure, energy, power, fuel economy, angle and cooking units, including local units such as Marla, Kanal, Tola, Masha and Ratti. It also converts world currencies using live exchange rates.

Swapzy does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not collect any personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Swapzy does not collect, store or access:

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your location (precise or approximate)
  • Your contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
  • Your advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • Usage analytics, crash analytics or behavior tracking

We do not operate any servers that receive data from the app.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, Swapzy saves the following information only on your device:

  • Conversion history – your recent conversions, so you can reuse them
  • Favorites – the unit and currency pairs you mark with a star
  • Settings – theme (light, dark or system), decimal places, number format, default currencies, calculator keypad and haptic feedback preferences
  • Cached exchange rates – the latest downloaded rates, so currency conversion also works offline
  • Home screen widget – the currency pair and rate shown on the widget

This information never leaves your device and is not shared with us or anyone else.

4. Currency Exchange Rates

To show up-to-date currency rates, Swapzy downloads them from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com), a third-party service.

This is a standard, secure HTTPS request that contains no personal information. Like any internet request, it reveals your device's IP address to that server, which handles it under its own privacy policy: https://www.exchangerate-api.com/terms

Unit conversions work fully offline and never use the internet.

5. App Permissions

Swapzy only uses the following permissions:

  • Internet access – to download currency exchange rates
  • Vibration – for optional light haptic feedback on key presses, which you can turn off in Settings

Swapzy does not request access to your location, storage, camera, microphone or contacts.

6. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking

Swapzy contains no advertisements, no analytics tools and no tracking software. We do not use cookies, we do not use your advertising ID, and we do not build any profile about you.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any user information with third parties, because Swapzy does not collect any personal information.

8. Data Security

All app data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security features. Exchange rates are downloaded over an encrypted HTTPS connection.

9. Data Deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can:

  • Clear your conversion history from inside the app at any time
  • Clear all app data from Android Settings → Apps → Swapzy → Storage → Clear storage
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently removes all history, favorites, settings and cached rates, subject to any device backup or restoration features

Swapzy does not maintain any online database of user accounts or usage records.

10. Children's Privacy

Swapzy is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the app, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

12. Your Consent

By installing and using Swapzy, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy.
